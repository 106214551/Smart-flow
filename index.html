What We Do

Our Solutions

We work with councils and industry organisations to build digital platforms that make cities work better. Our solutions improve transport, energy use and public services.

Smart Transport

We develop digital platforms that combine traffic, public transport and parking data. Councils can reduce congestion, plan better routes and help people travel more easily.

Learn more

Energy Monitoring

Our platforms track energy use in buildings, street lighting and public facilities. Clear reports help councils and businesses reduce energy waste, costs and emissions.

Learn more

Urban Services

We build platforms that help councils manage waste collection, maintenance and public infrastructure. Problems can be reported, tracked and resolved more quickly.

Learn more

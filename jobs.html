DIGITAL PLATFORMS

Front-End Web Developer

Smart Transport Platforms

Full-time
Salary $95,000–$110,000 + super Location Melbourne, VIC · hybrid Type Permanent

Build and maintain public-facing web interfaces for council transport dashboards, including live bus and tram arrivals, car-park availability and road-works notices. Your work must be fast, clear and accessible to residents and council staff.

Reference number
SF101
Reports to
Lead Front-End Developer, Digital Platforms team
Work arrangement
Two days on site; hybrid

Key responsibilities

  • Build responsive, accessible interfaces using HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript.
  • Turn designer wireframes into reusable front-end components.
  • Work with data engineers to display live transport feeds.
  • Test pages against WCAG 2.1 AA and resolve accessibility issues.
  • Take part in sprint planning, reviews and retrospectives.

Essential requirements

  1. Degree or diploma in web development, computer science or IT.
  2. At least two years' experience with HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript.
  3. Experience building responsive and accessible websites.
  4. Confidence using Git for version control.

Preferred requirements

  • Experience with web mapping libraries such as Leaflet or Mapbox.
  • Previous work on public-sector or council projects.
  • Familiarity with Jira and agile delivery.

Interested in this role? Include reference SF101 in your application.

DATA & INSIGHTS

Data Visualisation and UX Designer

Energy Monitoring

12-month contract
Salary $85,000–$100,000 + super Location Melbourne, VIC · hybrid Type Contract, option to extend

Design dashboards councils use to monitor energy consumption across public buildings, street lighting and EV charging networks. Turn thousands of sensor readings into charts and maps that facilities managers can understand and act on.

Reference number
SF102
Reports to
Head of Data & Insights
Work arrangement
Two days on site; hybrid

Key responsibilities

  • Produce wireframes and clickable prototypes for dashboard features.
  • Design charts, maps and alerts for non-technical council staff.
  • Run usability testing with council users and apply the findings.
  • Maintain the Smart-flow design system and component library.
  • Work alongside front-end developers from first sketch to launch.

Essential requirements

  1. Degree in design, human-computer interaction or a related field.
  2. A portfolio showing data-heavy or dashboard design work.
  3. Proficiency in Figma or a similar design tool.
  4. Working knowledge of colour contrast and accessibility in visual design.

Preferred requirements

  • Experience with Power BI, Tableau or D3.js.
  • Background in energy, utilities or sustainability.
  • Experience planning and running user research.

Interested in this role? Include reference SF102 in your application.

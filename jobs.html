Careers at Smart-flow

Help councils build the digital backbone of smarter cities.

Current vacancies

Smart-flow works with local councils and industry partners to design and build the digital platforms behind smart transport, energy monitoring and urban services. We are growing our Digital Platforms and Data & Insights teams, and looking for people who want to see their work used by real communities.

Two positions are open now. Read the details below, note the reference number, and apply online.

Why work with us

Smart-flow is a Melbourne-based team of about 40 engineers, designers and urban planners. Our platforms are used by more than a dozen Victorian councils, so the work you do here shows up in real streets and buildings.

What we offer

  • Hybrid working and flexible hours
  • $2,000 annual professional development budget
  • Two paid volunteering days a year with community partners
  • Modern office near Southern Cross Station

Questions?

Front-End Web Developer (Smart Transport Platforms)

Reference number
SF101
Salary
$95,000 to $110,000 per year plus superannuation
Reports to
Lead Front-End Developer, Digital Platforms team
Location
Melbourne, VIC (hybrid, two days on site)
Employment type
Full-time, permanent

About the role

You will build and maintain the public-facing web interfaces for the transport dashboards we deliver to councils: live bus and tram arrivals, car-park availability, road-works notices and more. Your work will be used every day by residents and council staff, so it has to be fast, clear and accessible to everyone.

Key responsibilities

  • Build responsive, accessible interfaces using HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript
  • Turn the designer wireframes into reusable front-end components
  • Work with our data engineers to display live transport feeds on the web
  • Test pages against WCAG 2.1 AA and fix accessibility issues
  • Take part in sprint planning, reviews and retrospectives

Essential requirements

  1. Degree or diploma in web development, computer science or IT
  2. At least two years' experience with HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript
  3. Demonstrated experience building responsive and accessible websites
  4. Confident using Git for version control

Preferable requirements

  • Experience with web mapping libraries such as Leaflet or Mapbox
  • Previous work on public-sector or council projects
  • Familiarity with Jira and agile delivery

Data Visualisation and UX Designer (Energy Monitoring)

Reference number
SF102
Salary
$85,000 to $100,000 per year plus superannuation
Reports to
Head of Data & Insights
Location
Melbourne, VIC (hybrid, two days on site)
Employment type
Full-time, 12-month contract with option to extend

About the role

You will design the dashboards councils use to monitor energy consumption across public buildings, street lighting and EV charging networks. The job is to turn thousands of sensor readings into charts and maps that a facilities manager can understand at a glance and act on the same day.

Key responsibilities

  • Produce wireframes and clickable prototypes for new dashboard features
  • Design charts, maps and alerts for non-technical council staff
  • Run usability testing sessions with council users and feed the results back to the team
  • Maintain the Smart-flow design system and component library
  • Work alongside front-end developers from first sketch to launch

Essential requirements

  1. Degree in design, human-computer interaction or a related field
  2. A portfolio showing data-heavy or dashboard design work
  3. Proficiency in Figma or a similar design tool
  4. Working knowledge of colour contrast and accessibility in visual design

Preferable requirements

  • Experience with Power BI, Tableau or D3.js
  • Background in the energy, utilities or sustainability sector
  • Experience planning and running user research

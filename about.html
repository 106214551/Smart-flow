Acknowledgement of Country

We respectfully acknowledge the Wurundjeri People of the Kulin Nation, the Traditional Owners of the land on which Swinburne University of Technology's Australian campuses stand. We pay our respects to their Elders past and present.

Why we included this

Our team studies a Swinburne course, so we wanted to recognise the Traditional Owners of the land where our university began. An Acknowledgement of Country recognises Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples as the Traditional Owners of the land and shows respect for their continuing connection to Country (Reconciliation Australia, n.d.).

It also fits the company we designed. Smart city projects change how land, streets and public spaces are used. CivicGrid Consulting therefore commits to:

  • talking with Traditional Owners early in every council project;
  • encouraging applications from Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples for all roles;
  • designing digital services that are accessible to everyone in the community.
